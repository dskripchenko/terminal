First release: `Terminal` (the xterm family's escape sequences into rows of cells: colours, styles, wide characters and clusters, scrolling regions, the alternate screen, modes, answers, OSC title/folder/links/marks, reflow on resize) and `TerminalView` (drawn in the DOM, only the rows in view; keys, input methods, pasting, selection, the mouse, links, fitting the element). Checked against xterm.js on sequences and on recorded sessions of real programs, and in Chromium and WebKit.
